Layer Map

  1. Goal. Mark water, minerals, and hazards, and log three temperatures.
  2. Move. WASD drives. Click the big map to cruise. The visor is the active sensor.
  3. Decide. Each layer paints only where you drive, and each one hides what the others show.
  4. Score. The meter is a claim until you end the survey. 60% passes. Careful marks can clear 90%.
